KiCad 9 Hardware Deep-Dive

MCU Data Logger — ATmega328P Acquisition Board

1. Overview & Objective

Designed to serve as a standalone embedded telemetry logger. The objective was to design a clean 2-layer PCB containing an 8-bit AVR microcontroller running at 16 MHz, a dedicated battery-backed real-time clock (DS1337) with its own 32.768 kHz quartz crystal, and dual high-capacity non-volatile EEPROMs (24LC1025) sharing an I2C bus with hardware address strap pins.

2. System & Components

MCU: ATmega328P-AU (TQFP-32)
RTC & Battery Backup: DS1337 + 32.768 kHz Quartz Crystal
Non-Volatile Storage: 2× Microchip 24LC1025 I2C EEPROM (256 KB)
Bus & Headers: I2C, UART, 6-pin ICSP, GPIO Header

3. Schematic, Layout & 3D Views

Schematic Sheet 1: MCU Core & Power
2-Layer PCB Top Layout

4. Design Process & Verification

  • Hierarchical Design: Partitioned schematic into clean logical blocks (MCU, RTC, EEPROM, and external connectors).
  • Bypass Capacitors: 0.1µF decoupling capacitors placed immediately adjacent to VCC and AVCC pins before routing to power rails.
  • Oscillator Isolation: Kept 16 MHz and 32.768 kHz crystal tracks as short as possible with tight loop area to prevent parasitic coupling.
  • Ground Return: Flooded board with continuous ground copper fills on both layers connected through vias.
  • Rules Verification: Zero Electrical Rules Check (ERC) violations and zero clearance Design Rules Check (DRC) violations.
  • Manufacturing Output: Generated standard Gerber RS-274X files, Excellon drill files, and silkscreen layers.

Engineering Methodology

PCB Design Workflow

The systematic, repeatable engineering process I follow for every board from concept to fabrication.

STEP 01

Requirements & Architecture

Define circuit requirements, operating voltages, component datasheets, and interface pinouts.

STEP 02

Schematic Capture

Organize hierarchical sheets, connect power flags, net labels, passive filters, and decoupling caps.

STEP 03

ERC Verification

Run Electrical Rules Check to detect unconnected pins, power port conflicts, and net anomalies.

STEP 04

Footprint Assignment

Match packages (SOIC, TQFP, 0805 passives) and draft custom footprints for non-standard modules.

STEP 05

Board Setup & Placement

Set board outline (Edge.Cuts), place connectors at edges, group by function, place bypass caps at ICs.

STEP 06

Net Classes & Routing

Define trace width and clearance rules. Route critical clocks and high-speed lines first with 45° corners.

STEP 07

Copper Zones & Pours

Pour continuous top and bottom ground planes (GND) with thermal relief spokes on pads for soldering.

STEP 08

DRC & Gerber Export

Run Design Rules Check, inspect 3D view for collisions, export RS-274X Gerbers and Excellon drill files.

Core Concepts

PCB Knowledge & Capabilities

Technical principles applied across schematic architecture, layout rules, and board bring-up.

Component Placement

Organizing floorplans by functional domain (microcontroller core, power management, storage, external headers) to minimize total trace length.

Power Routing

Allocating wider track widths for primary power rails (VCC, 3.3V) to reduce trace impedance, limit voltage drop, and ensure stable supply currents.

Decoupling Strategy

Placing 0.1µF ceramic bypass capacitors directly adjacent to power pins before connecting to main rails to suppress high-frequency switching noise.

Crystal Oscillator Isolation

Placing quartz crystals (16 MHz and 32.768 kHz) close to the microcontroller with symmetric routing and minimal loop area to prevent radiation and drift.

Grounding & Return Paths

Utilizing solid copper ground planes on top and bottom layers, avoiding split returns, and providing low-impedance paths for return currents.

Trace Width & Clearance

Configuring net classes with distinct clearance rules for signal, power, and high-density IC pins to prevent solder bridging and signal cross-talk.

Bus Architecture & Pull-ups

Implementing dedicated pull-up resistors on shared I2C bus lines (SDA/SCL) and isolated TX/RX UART lines for external modules like GPS and LTE modems.

ERC & DRC Rule Checking

Rigorous sign-off: resolving all schematic pin-type warnings in ERC and enforcing zero clearance errors, unconnected nets, or courtyard collisions in DRC.

Gerber & Drill Generation

Exporting industry-standard RS-274X Gerber layer packages (copper, mask, silkscreen, edge cuts) and Excellon drill files ready for fabrication houses.
